Photon number is not a conserved quantity. Photons can be created and destroyed arbitrarily through interaction with matter. In language of [[Statistical Mechanics|statistical mechanics]], the photons inside a region which is in [[Thermodynamic Equilibrium|thermodynamic equilibrium]] has zero [[Chemical Potential|chemical potential]] $\mu_{\gamma}$.
