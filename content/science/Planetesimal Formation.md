As @armitageAstrophysicsPlanetFormation2020 notes, the formation of (terrestrial) planets from micrometer-sized dust particles requires growth through at least 12 orders of magnitude in size scale ($\sim{10}^{-6}–10^6\ \mathrm{m}$). Forming [[Planetesimal|planetesimals]] is the first step in this seemingly arduous process.
