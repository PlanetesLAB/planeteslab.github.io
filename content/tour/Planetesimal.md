If we look back at the [[Definition of a planet|definition of a planet]], we discussed few criteria that determine the lowest size (or mass) limit to determine a body of planetary mass and established the debate is still ongoing. Likewise, defining what counts as a planetesimal is not straightforward either. For example, are all [[Minor Planet Taxonomy|minor planets]] planetesimals? There is also the notion of being a *primordial* planetesimal or a *remnant* of a planetesimal. 

I think it is constructive to restrict the usage of the term planetesimal to the bodies during the formation and evolutionary epoch. The [[The Solar System|Solar System]] today is a mature and stable system[^1]. Thus, one can argue that planetesimals don't exist today and most of the minor planets are plainly remnants of bodies that never progressed from planetesimal stage to a full planet.

[^1]: Declaring this actually requires a specific set of assumptions described [[The History of Solar System#The Solar System Today|here]].
