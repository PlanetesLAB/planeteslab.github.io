The Kinematic Local Standard of Rest or LSRK is an astronomical reference frame designed to represent the mean motion of material in the [[Solar Neighborhood|solar neighborhood]] first described by @gordonRadioObservations1975. Conceptually, in the LSRK frame, the average motion of the stars in the solar neighborhood is zero[^1]. By convention, the Sun moves relative to LSRK at $20\ \mathrm{km\ s}^{-1}$ towards $\mathrm{RA}\ 270 \degree$, $\mathrm{DEC}\ -30\degree$. 

[^1]: In practice, the observed average motion is different for different [[Stellar classification|spectral types]], which has historically justified using convenient rounded values for the solar motion relative to the LSR.
